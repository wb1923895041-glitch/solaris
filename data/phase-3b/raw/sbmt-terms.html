Small Body Mapping Tool

Terms and Conditions

Please acknowledge use of the Small Body Mapping Tool in your abstracts and publications. This courtesy helps us to demonstrate how the SBMT is being used and, therefore, contributes to the SBMT's continued availability, maintenance, and development. We are excited to see how the SBMT is being used by the community. Click here for a list of papers and abstracts that have described or used the SBMT.

Currently, the best reference to cite for the SBMT is Ernst et al., 2018, The Small Body Mapping Tool (SBMT) for Accessing, Visualizing, and Analyzing Spacecraft Data in Three Dimensions, LPSC 49, abstract no. 1043.

If you have used the SBMT to create or map structures, you can share your data with the community by adding them to the SBMT structures database. If you would like to share your data in this way, please email sbmt@jhuapl.edu.
